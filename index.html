StoreCheck

Saw a product in an ad? Paste the store link and see the red flags before you buy.

Tip: on the ad, tap the link, then copy the URL from your browser. A product link works best.

StoreCheck shows automated risk signals from public data (domain records, certificate logs and the store's own pages). It doesn't decide whether a business is legitimate. A new store can be honest, and an old one can still mislead. Use it as a starting point.
